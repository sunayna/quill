# How to Check Your Report Remarks

1. **REMARKS**
   - Complete all remarks in the school spreadsheet.
   - Go through the checklist and ensure your remarks follow the guidelines suggested.

2. **AI CHECK**
   - Paste the system prompt (`prompts/system_prompt.md`) as the first message — or into the system/instructions field, if the interface has one.
   - Upload `rubric/quillwarden_rubric.json`.
   - Upload the xlsx with that batch's remarks.

3. **CORRECTION**
   - Review the issues identified for each student.
   - Correct the remarks yourself in the spreadsheet.
   - Upload the corrected spreadsheet and type: *"Recheck the revised remarks against the uploaded rubric. Show only unresolved issues. Do not rewrite any remark."*

4. **SUBMISSION**
   - Submit the final spreadsheet once all critical and required issues have been resolved.

---

*The AI identifies possible issues. The teacher remains responsible for factual accuracy and final wording.*
