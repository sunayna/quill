# Quillwarden — Teacher Checklist (v1.0)

*Review before submitting. The teacher makes the final call on any revision.*

## Content
- [ ] At least **one specific, observable strength**, supported by an example where appropriate (not "wonderful student")
- [ ] Claims are backed by evidence (participation, work samples, projects, records)
- [ ] Academic claims — improved, declined, *or general* ("performs well across all subjects," "exceeds grade-level expectations") — match actual academic records
- [ ] At least **one specific and actionable growth point** stating what the student should practise or improve
- [ ] No more than 2–3 development points total
- [ ] Balanced — not all praise or all criticism, unless a strengths-only remark is intentionally permitted by school policy
- [ ] Remark describes **observable school behaviour**, not personality judgements (e.g. "submits work on time," not "is lazy" or "is a shy person")

## Accuracy
- [ ] Correct student name used **throughout** (no other student's name present)
- [ ] Pronouns are consistent and verified against roster — **never guessed from name**
- [ ] Awards, roles, clubs, competitions are verified, not assumed
- [ ] Attendance mentioned only if it affects learning — no private reasons given

## Tone & Language
- [ ] No casual phrasing ("keep shining," "a joy to teach," "lively vibes")
- [ ] No sentimental/promotional language ("everyone adores her," "destined for greatness")
- [ ] No inflated praise unless evidenced ("exceptional," "outstanding," "perfect")
- [ ] No repeated "must / needs to / should" — use varied constructive phrasing
- [ ] Closing is brief and restrained (not "Reach for the stars!")

## Safeguarding & Privacy — CRITICAL
- [ ] No medical, diagnostic, therapy, or medication information
- [ ] No clinical/diagnostic wording ("dysregulated," "attention deficit") — describe behaviour instead
- [ ] No family, home, sibling, or financial details
- [ ] No comments on hygiene, sleep, eating, clothing, or home routines
- [ ] No humiliating or shaming language

## Equity
- [ ] No comparison to classmates, siblings, parents, or "average students"
- [ ] No superiority claims ("best in class," "sets him apart")
- [ ] No assumptions based on gender, culture, disability, or background

## Consistency & Mechanics
- [ ] No contradictions (same trait as both strength and weakness, unexplained)
- [ ] No duplicated sentences, paragraphs, or closings
- [ ] Logical connectors used correctly (however, although, additionally)
- [ ] Grammar, spelling, punctuation, spacing checked
- [ ] British/Indian school spelling used consistently (organisation, practise/practice, etc.)
- [ ] Programme/subject/sport/event names capitalised per school style (Khoj, Eco Club, Sports Day, etc.)

## Length
- [ ] Under **1,000 characters** (including spaces)
- [ ] Roughly **100–150 words**, clear and readable

## Final Sign-off
- [ ] All flags above reviewed
- [ ] Final correction made and approved by the teacher
